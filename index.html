ABOUT THE HOTEL

A calm place to stay
in Gombe

Cactus Hotels sits inside the Abubakar Habu Hashidu Housing Estate, a short distance from the centre of Gombe. Every room is made up fresh before arrival, with crisp white linen finished by our branded bed runner, and the quiet you need after a day of work or travel.

Whether you are in town for business, a ceremony or a weekend of rest, our rooms are kept simple, clean and comfortable, and our team is on hand to make your stay easy.
